THE PAIRTHE PAIRTwo groups of stocks: a LONG leg you would own and a SHORT leg you would sell against it. Each pair is oriented so the long leg is the one with the stronger analyst revisions.
ENGINE 1 · ANALYST REVISIONSENGINE 1 · ANALYST REVISIONSAre analysts raising or cutting price targets and estimates? Measured here as breadth: the share of names in a group being raised minus the share being cut. A leading signal, weekly.
ENGINE 2 · BUSINESS INFLECTIONENGINE 2 · BUSINESS INFLECTIONIs the business itself improving (margins, growth, returns, leverage), from the financial statements? Measured as breadth: share of names inflecting up minus share deteriorating. A leading signal, weekly.
ENGINE 3 · 13FENGINE 3 · 13F FUND FLOWSWhat your watchlist of respected funds did last quarter. A lagging, confirming signal: it arrives about 45 days after quarter end.
LAST 13 WEEKSTRAJECTORY · LAST 13 WEEKSTwo small charts side by side. If the left one (signals) has moved and the right one (prices) has not, the divergence is not priced yet.
SHARE-PRICE PERFORMANCEPRICE RATIOHow the long leg's share price has performed against the short leg's. This is the 'share price performance' view of the pair.
VALUATIONVALUATIONIs the long leg already expensive against the short leg, compared with this pair's own history?
ENGINE 4 · FACTOR MODELENGINE 4 · FACTOR MODELYour 14-factor regression applied to the spread (long minus short). It answers: is this pair a real stock-specific bet, or a disguised bet on momentum, rates, oil, etc.?
